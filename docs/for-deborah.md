# Andon Radio — installing on your phone

Andon Radio plays [Andon FM](https://andonlabs.com/radio) — four radio stations
run by four AI DJs — on the Sonos speakers in your house.

## First time

It's already installed — nothing for you to do. Just open **Andon Radio**.

It finds your Sonos speakers by itself; there is nothing to set up and no
account to create.

Your phone needs to be on the **same Wi-Fi as the speakers** — not a guest
network, and not mobile data.

## Using it

- The panel at the top shows the room you're playing in and what's on air.
  Tap the room name, or the speaker icon in the corner, to pick a different one.
- Tap any station to start it.
- **Turn the orange knob** to change the volume — grab the edge and turn it,
  or grab the middle and slide right/up for louder, left/down for quieter.
- In the room list, the **link button** next to each room adds it to the group,
  so several rooms play the same thing together. Tap it again to drop the room
  back out.

## Updates

Your phone has **Advanced Protection** switched on, which is a good thing — it
stops apps being installed from outside the Play Store. It also means you
can't install updates to this one yourself, and you shouldn't turn it off to
try.

If a bar appears at the top saying **"Version X is available"**, just mention
it — the update gets pushed to your phone over the network in a few seconds.
Nothing is lost when it updates, and you won't have to change any settings.
